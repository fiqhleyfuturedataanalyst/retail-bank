03 CASH-FLOW FIRST

MaxiHome Ezy

Lower commitments now, full instalments later.

YEARS 1–5 lower monthly commitment
→
YEAR 6+ full instalment begins
HOW IT WORKS
A stepped-up repayment structure with lower monthly commitments during the first five years.
BEST FOR
Customers who want more cash in hand for moving, renovation or other early home-ownership costs.
FLEXIBILITY
You can still make extra payments to help save interest.
WHAT TO CONSIDER
Repayments step up later, so make sure the full instalment from year six fits your longer-term budget.
